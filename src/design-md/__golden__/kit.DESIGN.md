# ACME

## Overview

ACME is a sample brand for testing, not a real company. Its tagline is “Tools that work the first time.”

- **Tagline:** Tools that work the first time.
- **Tone:** Direct, Plainspoken, Assured
- **Personality:** Practical, Confident, No-nonsense
- **Audience:** Tool users seeking reliable, first-time performance.

## Colors

- **Acme Red (#d7263d):** Use for primary actions and the wordmark. Allocate 10% of the page’s color use to Acme Red.
- **Ink (#1b1b1e):** Use for text and dark surfaces. Allocate 25% of the page’s color use to Ink.
- **Paper (#f7f4ef):** Use for the page background. Allocate 50% of the page’s color use to Paper.
- **Steel (#5c6b73):** Use for secondary text and borders. Allocate 10% of the page’s color use to Steel.
- **Sun (#f4b942):** Use sparingly for highlights. Allocate 5% of the page’s color use to Sun.

## Typography

- **Helvetica Neue:** Use Helvetica Neue for display, headings, body text and captions. Use Bold for display and headings; Regular for body text and captions.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Use short, declarative sentences.
- Lead with the practical product benefit.
- Avoid jargon and ornate language.
- Avoid hedging straightforward benefit claims.
- Maintain clear space on every side of the wordmark: height of the A in the wordmark.
- Minimum width of the wordmark: 96 px on screen / 24 mm in print.
- Do not stretch, slant or rotate the wordmark.
- Do not place the wordmark on Acme Red or on a busy photo.
- Bold for display and headings, Regular for body and captions.
- Body text sets at 16 px on 24 px.
- Body text is left aligned.
- Body text uses 60 to 75 characters a line.
- Use a 6 px corner radius.
- Use 12 px padding at the top and bottom.
- Use 24 px padding at the left and right.
- Use 16 px Bold labels.
- Use Acme Red fill and a Paper label.
- Use one primary button per screen.
- Use no fill.
- Use a 1.5 px Ink border.
- Use an Ink label.
